## Introduction

I came across this little growing kit while I was out and bought it on impulse.

<a href="https://link.amazon/B08YNS2Uv"><img src="/blog/mame-bonsai/product.webp" alt="Seishin Pottery Mini Bonsai Growing Capsule" width="240" loading="lazy"></a>

I guess spending so much time tinkering with machines makes me want to spend time with something alive from nature.

## What Will I Get?

The seeds come inside a capsule, and there are four varieties in total.

<img src="/blog/mame-bonsai/capsule-varieties.webp" alt="The four bonsai varieties available in the capsules" width="800" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

I was hoping for a **Japanese black pine**, but didn't think I would get the one I wanted.

And then I did! So happy!

<img src="/blog/mame-bonsai/tane.webp" alt="The capsule with Japanese black pine seeds" width="320" loading="lazy">

## Planting the Seeds

The kit includes seeds, a tiny pot, and soil, so I planted them right away. Grow well!

## Sprouting

About ten days later, I woke up to a new little life sprouting. So cute!

When I got home that evening, another one had popped up beside it!

<img src="/blog/mame-bonsai/hatuga.webp" alt="Japanese black pine seedlings sprouting" width="320" loading="lazy">

## Conclusion

The capsule toy cost 500 yen, so I wasn't sure the seeds would sprout. I was glad to see them come up so healthy.

I'll keep watching them and hope to write more updates as they grow.
